weil, obwohl, deshalb, trotzdem

In the game: you get clause A, clause B and a connector. Type one sentence that uses both clauses and the connector. Only the order of the words changes, and the commas go where they belong.

1. Two jobs, two grammar types

Conjunction
starts a subordinate clause		Adverb
counts as position 1
Reasonweil = becausedeshalb = therefore, that's why
Contrastobwohl = althoughtrotzdem = nevertheless, anyway
  • weil and obwohl introduce the reason / obstacle itself. deshalb and trotzdem introduce the result / action that follows from it.
  • deshalb has the same grammar as deswegen and darum.

2. weil and obwohl: the verb goes to the end

  • Main clause first: Ich bleibe zu Hause, weil es regnet.
  • weil/obwohl clause first: Weil es regnet, bleibe ich zu Hause. The main clause then starts with its verb (position 1), then the subject.
  • A comma always separates the two clauses.
  • Verb at the end means the conjugated verb. With a modal or the Perfekt that is the last word of the clause: weil ich früh aufstehen muss, weil er den Schlüssel verloren hat. A separable verb joins up again: der Zug fällt aus → weil der Zug ausfällt.

3. deshalb and trotzdem: the verb comes right after them

  • Es regnet, trotzdem gehe ich spazieren. (trotzdem = position 1, verb = position 2, then the subject)
  • Es regnet, deshalb bleibe ich zu Hause.
  • They can also sit in the middle of the second clause: Es regnet, ich gehe trotzdem spazieren. The game accepts this too. A period also works: Es regnet. Trotzdem gehe ich spazieren.

4. Word order at a glance

ConnectorPatternExample

obwohlmain clause, obwohl + subject … verbIch gehe zur Arbeit, obwohl ich krank bin.
deshalbclause A, deshalb + verb + subject …Ich bin müde, deshalb gehe ich früh ins Bett.
trotzdemclause A, trotzdem + verb + subject …Ich bin krank, trotzdem gehe ich zur Arbeit.

5. Common mistakes

  • weil + verb in position 2 (weil es regnet nicht / weil ich bin müde) is common in speech but wrong in writing and exams.
  • trotzdem + subject first: Es regnet, trotzdem ich gehe spazieren ✗. Say trotzdem gehe ich spazieren ✓.
  • Mixing up the pairs: Ich bin müde, weil ich gehe ins Bett ✗. The tired part is the reason (weil ich müde bin), going to bed is the result (deshalb gehe ich ins Bett).
  • Quick test: if the word is followed by the subject, it is weil/obwohl and the verb goes last. If it is followed by the verb, it is deshalb/trotzdem.
