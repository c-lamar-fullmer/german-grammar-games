Adjective endings

In the game: an adjective before a noun gets an ending. Which ending depends on three things: the word before the adjective (the type), the case and the gender of the noun (or plural).

1. Which type? Look at the word before the adjective

Weakder, die, das, dieser, jeder, welcher, alleder kleine Hund
Mixedein, kein, mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euerein kleiner Hund
Strongnothing, or viele, einige, wenige, mehrerekleine Hunde, kalter Kaffee

The idea: one word in the phrase must show the case and gender. If the article does it (der, dem, den), the adjective only needs -e or -en. If it does not (ein, no article), the adjective does it.

3. Shortcuts

  • Weak: only -e in the Nominativ (m, f, n) and in the Akkusativ feminine and neuter. Everything else is -en.
  • Mixed = weak, except where ein shows nothing: Nom masculine (ein kleiner Hund), Nom and Akk neuter (ein kleines Kind). Only those three cells change.
  • Strong: the adjective takes the ending of der/die/das: der Kaffee → kalter Kaffee, dem Wasser → kaltem Wasser, der Milch → kalter Milch.
  • Exception: Genitiv masculine and neuter without an article: trotz kalten Wetters (the noun already shows -s).
  • In the plural, the only strong endings that differ are Nom/Akk -e, Dat -en and Gen -er. After viele, einige, wenige, mehrere the adjective is strong too: viele neue Bücher.

Use the Learning steps in the game: weak first (Steps 1–2), then mixed (3–4), then strong (5–6), then all three together (7) and finally the Genitiv (8). Words and adjectives are in the Vocabulary tab.
